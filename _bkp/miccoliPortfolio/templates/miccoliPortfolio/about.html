{% load static %} Sandro Miccoli - About me

Hello, i'm sandro.

Creative technologist, educator and digital performer. Works at studio Abstracto creating interactive installations and immersive experiences. Also collaborates with collectives, artists and companies in creative technology.

Participated with projects, installations, performances and workshops at Battery Dance Festival (New York, United States), International Conference on Live Coding (Morelia, Mexico), Amsterdam Light Festival (Amsterdam, Netherlands), FILE – Electronic Language International Festival (São Paulo, Brazil), AnimaFest (Zagreb, Croatia), Biennial and Festival of Digital Art (Belo Horizonte, Brazil) e Reconvexo (Cachoeira, Brazil).

Also works as an Educational Technology consultant being a Google Certified Educator (Level 1 and 2), helping companies and schools on integrating Google Education in their workflows.

Graduated in Information Systems at UFMG (Federal University of Minas Gerais). Worked as a researcher at the MediaLab Amsterdam (current Digital Society School), in the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences (HvA). Worked as a researcher and game designer at Fields of View, Bangalore, India, as an intern via HvA. Back to Brazil, received a scholarship to research digital art and education at the LabFront, Escola Guignard, UEMG.
