<p>Live visuals for the premiere of “Grito de Gaia”, a concert dance created by <a href="https://www.instagram.com/conservatoriomineirodedanca/">Conservatório Mineiro de Dança</a>.</p>

<p>“Grito de Gaia” (can be translated to “Scream of Gaia” but maybe “Cry of Gaia” is more appropriate) is a dance concert that creates discussions around the relationship of Man and Earth, emphasizing the aesthetics of exuberance and scarcity of Nature. </p>

<p>My role in the project was creating the visuals that were projected behind the dancers in a 10x7 meters cyclorama on the Great Theater of Sesc Palladium in Belo Horizonte.</p>
